# Content Models

## 1. Status System
All primary content models will utilize the following initial status values:
- **Draft**
- **Published**
- **Archived**

*(Note: Unnecessary workflow states will not be added to keep the system simple and focused.)*

## 2. Article Content Model
The Article model is designed for blog posts, tutorials, and standard editorial content.
- **Title**
- **Slug**
- **Excerpt**
- **Content**
- **Cover Image**
- **Category**
- **Tags**
- **Author**
- **SEO Title**
- **SEO Description**
- **Status**
- **Published Date**

## 3. Case Study Content Model
The Case Study model is independent of the Article model and tailored specifically for showcasing projects and client work.
- **Title**
- **Slug**
- **Client**
- **Industry**
- **Services**
- **Cover Image**
- **Overview**
- **Challenge**
- **Solution**
- **Results**
- **Technology**
- **Gallery**
- **Testimonial**
- **SEO Title**
- **SEO Description**
- **Status**
- **Published Date**

## 4. Digital Asset Content Model
The Digital Asset model is designed for distributing or eventually selling resources (website templates, UI resources, free resources, downloadable files, etc.).
- **Title**
- **Slug**
- **Description**
- **Cover Image**
- **Type**
- **Category**
- **File / Download URL**
- **Price**
- **License**
- **Status**
- **Published Date**

*(Note: The model supports both Free and Paid assets, though payment functionality is out of scope for early development phases.)*
